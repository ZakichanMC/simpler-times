# Forward And Back

The status of this project is [FINISHED].

I'm really proud of myself for this one. This was a submission to the 2025 Brainless Mini Game Jam, Jan 4 - Jan 11. This is my second ever game jam.

Forward and Back is a 2D platformer metroidvania. You're a strange cave creature who has been mysteriously sent to the future, and have to turn back time to go to "simpler times" when the world didn't move as fast.

This project was rough at times. It is certainly the more impressive of my two game jam entries, and that comes at a cost. I put the game ahead of all my other responsibilities and by the end it was a slog to get over the finish line.

I'm really glad I participated and saw the jam through to the end. I learned that finishing is the worst part of gamedev. I learned that you should, in fact, NOT go with an idea that requires 4 different art styles, therefore 4 different versions of every single object, to make work. Especially not if it's a 7-day jam. I learned that I CAN finish games, despite what the annoying little runt in my head wants to tell me. I learned that gamedev is freaking awesome. And really, really hard. And that I want to keep making games.

Thanks for reading, and happy gamedev-ing.
